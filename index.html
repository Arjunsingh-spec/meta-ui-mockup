Campaign budget optimization
Set one budget for the whole campaign and let delivery decide how it is spent across ad sets.

Meta's platform floor is 1 to 5 dollars a day depending on objective, not a fixed 50. Anything technically valid but very low relative to your ad set count and target cost should still be reviewed.

Flag if far below or above recent cost per result rather than accepting any positive number.

Requires a value parameter on the optimization event. Do not accept this goal without one.
